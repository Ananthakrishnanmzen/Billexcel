Reconcile against GSTR-2B

GSTR-2B is the government's record of what your suppliers declared selling to you. Upload your GSTR-2B export (Excel or CSV, downloaded from the GST portal) to catch missing invoices, wrong tax rates, and duplicates before they cost you money.

This app cannot fetch GSTR-2B from the government portal directly — that data sits behind a login the portal does not expose to browser apps. Download it yourself from the portal and drop it here; the matching below then runs locally.
